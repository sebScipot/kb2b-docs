## Quick answers for procurement

For an initial security and compliance evaluation — current state, stated plainly:

- **Hosting and storage run in the United States today.** The app runs on Fly.io (Virginia) and data lives in AWS `us-east-1` (databases, files, queues) and Pinecone `us-east-1` (vector index). Migration of this infrastructure to EU regions is in progress.
- **What runs in the EU today:** meeting audio capture and transcription (Recall.ai, Frankfurt) and product analytics (PostHog EU cloud).
- **Retention today:** the recording provider's copy of meeting audio is deleted automatically after 7 days. Everything else (documents, transcripts, facts, chat history) is kept until you delete it from the app — there is no automatic expiry. See the [Recording policy](/en/legal/recording-policy) for what deletion covers today.
- **Recording consent documented** — see [Recording policy](/en/legal/recording-policy).
- **DPA: in preparation** — email [legal@kb2b.app](mailto:legal@kb2b.app) for status and timeline.
- **Security call with a responsible party** — request one by emailing [legal@kb2b.app](mailto:legal@kb2b.app) with subject "Security call".

## Current sub-processors

All customer data flows go through the services below. The AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) do not train models on data sent through their APIs, per their commercial API terms; they may retain inputs briefly for abuse monitoring.

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Customer data | Region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fly.io | Application hosting | All application traffic; kb2b database | US (Virginia) |
| AWS | Databases, file storage, queues, backups | Documents, transcripts, meeting audio archive, chat content | US (`us-east-1`) |
| Pinecone | Vector search | Fact embeddings and identifiers (no raw text) | US (`us-east-1`) |
| OpenAI | Fact extraction, synthesis, embeddings | Document excerpts, facts, queries | US |
| Anthropic | Chat answers and summaries | Questions, facts, transcripts | US |
| Recall.ai | Meeting audio capture and transcription | Raw audio, transcripts (auto-deleted after 7 days on their side) | EU (Frankfurt) |
| Stripe | Billing | Email, organization id | Global |
| Resend | Transactional email | Email addresses, login codes, invitations | US |
| PostHog | Product analytics | Usage events tied to user identity | EU cloud |
| Tavily | Web research in chat (optional feature, can be disabled) | Search queries derived from chat | US |
| Handinger | Fetching user-submitted URLs (optional feature, can be disabled) | URLs submitted by users | US |

Sign-in uses Google or GitHub as OAuth identity providers (basic profile only).

## Workspace isolation

Each kb2b workspace lives in its own **POT** (Knowledge Pot — the account's knowledge container). POTs are isolated at the database level by `workspace_id`. **There is no way for one workspace to query another workspace's data** — neither accidentally, nor via prompt injection.
