## Quick answers for procurement

For an initial security and compliance evaluation — current state, stated plainly:

- **Primary infrastructure and data storage and processing run in the European Union.** The app runs on Fly.io in Frankfurt (`fra`); data lives in AWS `eu-central-1` (databases, files, queues) and Pinecone `eu-central-1` (vector index). Meeting audio capture and transcription (Recall.ai, `eu-central-1`) and product analytics (PostHog EU cloud) also run in the EU. Transitional note: frozen US backups are scheduled for deletion by 2026-08-16.
- **What still processes in the US:** LLM inference (OpenAI, Anthropic) under their commercial API terms, and transactional email (Resend) — see the sub-processor table below.
- **Retention and deletion:** one policy per data type in the [section below](#retention-and-deletion). The short version: meeting audio expires on its own (7 days at the provider); everything else is kept until you delete it, and deletion is real — cascading to facts, embeddings, files and the provider's copy, out of backups within 35 days at most.
- **Recording consent documented** — see [Recording policy](/en/legal/recording-policy).
- **DPA: in preparation** — email [legal@kb2b.app](mailto:legal@kb2b.app) for status and timeline.
- **Security call with a responsible party** — request one by emailing [legal@kb2b.app](mailto:legal@kb2b.app) with subject "Security call".

## Retention and deletion

One policy, per data type. "Delete" means actually delete: the data leaves primary storage immediately and leaves backups by natural expiry, within 35 days at most.

| Data | Retention | Deletion |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting audio — provider copy (Recall.ai) | 7 days (168 h), automatic | Also deleted immediately when you delete the meeting |
| Archived audio and raw transcript (kb2b storage) | For as long as the meeting exists | Deleted when you delete the meeting |
| Documents, facts and embeddings | Until you delete them | Cascading: deleting a document or meeting erases its facts, their relationships and their vector-index embeddings |
| Chat history | Until you delete the conversation | Stored content is erased when you delete the conversation |
| Backups | Database replica: 24 h window. Files: backup versions of deleted objects expire after 30 days. Knowledge base: 35-day restore window | Automatic expiry — no indefinite copies |
| Usage events (internal telemetry, no document content) | 90 days | Automatic expiry; only billing aggregates are kept |
| Account / workspace | While the account is active | Closure and erasure on request to [legal@kb2b.app](mailto:legal@kb2b.app); self-serve full account deletion and data export are in development |

Operational detail on meeting deletion in the [Recording policy](/en/legal/recording-policy).

## Current sub-processors

All customer data flows go through the services below. The AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic) do not train models on data sent through their APIs, per their commercial API terms; they may retain inputs briefly for abuse monitoring.

| Sub-processor | Purpose | Customer data | Region | Transfer safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fly.io | Application hosting | All application traffic; kb2b database | EU (Frankfurt, `fra`) | [DPA](https://fly.io/documents/) with SCCs · [DPF](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list) active |
| AWS | Databases, file storage, queues, backups | Documents, transcripts, meeting audio archive, chat content | EU (`eu-central-1`) | [DPA](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/gdpr-center/) with SCCs · [DPF](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/participant/5776) active |
| Pinecone | Vector search | Fact embeddings and identifiers (no raw text) | EU (`eu-central-1`) | [DPA](https://www.pinecone.io/legal/data-processing-addendum/) with SCCs |
| OpenAI | Fact extraction, synthesis, embeddings | Document excerpts, facts, queries | US | [DPA](https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum/) with SCCs · contract with OpenAI Ireland Ltd. |
| Anthropic | Chat answers and summaries | Questions, facts, transcripts | US | [DPA](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum) with SCCs · contract with Anthropic Ireland, Limited |
| Recall.ai (Hyperdoc Inc.) | Meeting audio capture and transcription | Raw audio, transcripts (auto-deleted after 7 days on their side, and immediately when you delete the meeting) | EU (Frankfurt, `eu-central-1`) | [DPA](https://www.recall.ai/data-processing-agreement) with SCCs |
| Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd.) | Billing | Email, organization id | Global | [DPA](https://stripe.com/legal/dpa) with SCCs · [DPF](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/participant/10014) active |
| Resend (Plus Five Five, Inc.) | Transactional email | Email addresses, login codes, invitations | US | [DPA](https://resend.com/legal/dpa) with SCCs · [DPF](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list) active (EU/UK) |
| PostHog | Product analytics | Usage events tied to user identity | EU cloud (Frankfurt) | [DPA](https://posthog.com/dpa) with SCCs · [DPF](https://www.dataprivacyframework.gov/list) active |

Sign-in uses Google or GitHub as OAuth identity providers (basic profile only).

## Workspace isolation

Each kb2b workspace lives in its own **POT** (Knowledge Pot — the account's knowledge container). POTs are isolated at the database level by `workspace_id`. **There is no way for one workspace to query another workspace's data** — neither accidentally, nor via prompt injection.
